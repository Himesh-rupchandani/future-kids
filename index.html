Our Mission

To spark DICE (Design Thinking, Innovation, Creativity and Entrepreneurship) and foster a deep interest in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) in children and young adults through hands on practical learning by "Making".

Our Vision

To develop hands-on learning interactive program and educational kit and sets to revolutionize education system by encouraging children to build, construct and get creative with our interactive educational Kit and not solely learning through books.

Our Programs

The curriculum design at Future Kids is developmentally based and focuses on the individual child's social, emotional, and intellectual needs and is oriented on three age groups

AGES 3+

Inspire Explorers

Where Kids Discover The Joy Of Education

Grade levels: Nursery, Kg1 and Kg2

A Preprimary Program for little hands using "Build, Play and Learn" strategy. Building Brilliance via STEM Play and Curriculum for curious minds ages 3 and up.

Introduction to Math and Literacy via kinesthetic Learning using Lego-like blocks specifically designed for smaller hands. These bricks allow simple construction and encourage creativity and imagination.

AGES 4+

Inspire Builders

Stepping Stone to STEM

Grade levels: Std 1 to Std 4

We provide easy-to-use Future Kids builder kit starting ages 4+ that introduce young students to robotics.

Students will be able to build unlimited endless models belonging to different themes. It will help them develop interest and comprehend theoretical science and mathematics which is introduced in school at a very early age.

AGES 8+

Inspire Freshmen

Building Motorized Machines

Grade levels: Std 5 to Std 8

Expand your child's horizons in the field of Robotics by building motorized machines.

Our instructor-led STEM curriculum will provide a multi-sensory experience like never before. Children will explore mechanical, mathematical, physical and logical aspects of machinery by building real life motorized machines.

AGES 13+

Inspire Scientipreneur

Developing R&D and Entrepreneurial Mindset

Grade levels: Std 9, Std 10, Std 11, Std 12

Tools used: Inspire Freshman Kit and Lego Digital Designer Software

Course Duration: 10-Session Year-Round (1 Session Per week)

Duration of Each Session: 1 Hour each

Program Strategy: Helping Kids discover themselves by toying with technology and becoming leaders of 21st Century. Getting them ready for research development mindset by guiding them Ideate, Apply Strategies, and create STEM Project.

AGES 8+

Block Programming

Grade levels: Std 5 to Std 9

Code your story with IBM's Blockly platform & Code.Org

  • One-on-One Sessions to enhance, elevate core coding concepts in S.T.E.M. in a simple fun way
  • Develop logical, decision-making skills with theme-based learning
IBM Blockly Code.org 1-on-1 Sessions
