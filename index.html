Our Mission

To spark DICE (Design Thinking, Innovation, Creativity and Entrepreneurship) and foster a deep interest in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) in children and young adults through hands on practical learning by "Making".

👁️

Our Vision

To develop hands-on learning interactive program and educational kit and sets to revolutionize education system by encouraging children to build, construct and get creative with our interactive educational Kit and not solely learning through books.

🎓 Age-Appropriate Curriculum

Our Programs

The curriculum design at Future Kids is developmentally based and focuses on the individual child's social, emotional, and intellectual needs across age groups

🧒
AGES 3+

Inspire Explorers

Inspire Explorers

Where Kids Discover The Joy Of Education

🎓 Grade levels: Nursery, Kg1 and Kg2

A Preprimary Program for little hands using "Build, Play and Learn" strategy. Introduction to Math and Literacy via kinesthetic learning using Lego-like blocks specifically designed for smaller hands.

🔧
AGES 4+

Inspire Builders

Inspire Builders

Stepping Stone to STEM

🎓 Grade levels: Std 1 to Std 4

Easy-to-use Future Kids builder kit for ages 4+ introducing young students to robotics. Build unlimited themed models while developing interest in theoretical science and mathematics.

⚙️
AGES 8+

Inspire Freshmen

Inspire Freshmen

Building Motorized Machines

🎓 Grade levels: Std 5 to Std 8

Expand horizons in Robotics by building motorized machines! Instructor-led STEM curriculum providing multi-sensory experiences exploring mechanical, mathematical, physical, and logical aspects of machinery.

🧑‍🔬
AGES 13+

Inspire Scientipreneur

Inspire Scientipreneur

Developing R&D and Entrepreneurial Mindset

🎓 Grade levels: Std 9, 10, 11, 12

Tools: Inspire Freshman Kit + Lego Digital Designer

Duration: 10-Session Year-Round

Each Session: 1 Hour per week

💻
AGES 8+

Block Programming

Block Programming

🎓 Grade levels: Std 5 to Std 9

Code your story with IBM's Blockly platform & Code.Org

  • ✅ One-on-One Sessions to enhance core coding concepts in S.T.E.M. in a simple fun way
  • ✅ Develop logical, decision-making skills with theme-based learning
🧱 IBM Blockly 🌐 Code.org 👤 1-on-1
