Our Philosophy

The DICE Framework

We nurture young minds through our unique DICE approach — Design, Innovation, Creativity, and Entrepreneurship.

Empowering kids to think critically and solve real-world problems through structured design processes.

D
I

Our Mission

To spark DICE (Design Thinking, Innovation, Creativity, and Entrepreneurship) and foster a deep interest in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) in children and young adults through hands-on practical learning by "Making".

Our Vision

To develop hands-on interactive learning programs and educational kits that revolutionize education by encouraging children to build, construct, and get creative — not solely learning through books.

Our Programs

Programs for Every Age

Our curriculum is developmentally designed to match your child's social, emotional, and intellectual needs across age groups.

Ages 3+

Inspire Explorers

Nursery, KG1 & KG2

Build Play Learn

A preprimary program for little hands using "Build, Play and Learn" strategy. Introduction to Math & Literacy via kinesthetic learning using Lego-like blocks designed for smaller hands.

Ages 4+

Inspire Builders

Std 1 to Std 4

Robotics Intro Builder Kit

Easy-to-use STEM builder kits introducing young students to robotics. Build unlimited themed models and develop early interest in theoretical science and mathematics through construction.

Ages 8+

Inspire Freshmen

Std 5 to Std 8

Motorized Machines

Expand horizons in Robotics by building motorized machines! Instructor-led STEM curriculum providing multi-sensory experiences exploring mechanical, mathematical, physical, and logical aspects of machinery.

Ages 13+

Inspire Scientipreneur

Std 9 to Std 12

R&D Entrepreneur

Developing R&D and entrepreneurial mindset using Inspire Freshman Kit and Lego Digital Designer. Year-round 10-session program guiding kids to ideate, apply strategies, and create real STEM projects.

Ages 8+

Block Programming

Std 5 to Std 9

IBM Blockly Code.org

Code your story with IBM's Blockly platform and Code.org! One-on-one sessions to enhance core coding concepts in STEM through simple, fun, theme-based learning that builds logical thinking.

Ages 10+

AI & Robotics Pro

Advanced Program

AI/ML Arduino

Dive into the future with Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and advanced robotics using Arduino & Raspberry Pi. Build smart robots, AI-powered projects, and compete in national championships!
